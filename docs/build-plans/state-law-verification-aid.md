## CA — California (seed: no)

**Pass claims:** p1: Y — Cal. Ins. Code § 10123.208 / Health & Safety Code
§ 1367.007 (AB 2180), eff. Jan 1, 2025. p2: N. p3: N.

**What the primary sources show:**
- AB 2180 (2023–2024 session) is a real bill. Legislative Counsel's Digest:
  would require health plans/insurers/PBMs administering pharmacy benefits
  to count amounts paid by the enrollee/insured **or a third-party patient
  assistance program** toward cost-sharing — **but only for enrollees with a
  chronic disease or terminal illness**, only for policies
  issued/amended/delivered/renewed on or after **January 1, 2025**, with a
  **January 1, 2035 repeal** and a DMHC/CDI impact report due 2034.
- CalMatters' tracker lists the bill's **Current Status: "In Committee."**
  The 2023–2024 session has ended; the official history shows no
  Governor action.
- Note the likely origin of p1's "eff. Jan 1, 2025": that date is the
  *bill's prospective applicability date*, which appears in the digest
  regardless of whether the bill passed.

**Links:**
- Official status/text: https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=202320240AB2180
- Tracker status: https://calmatters.digitaldemocracy.org/bills/ca_202320240ab2180

## IA — Iowa (seed: yes)

**Pass claims:** p1: N (implied — not listed). p2: Y, eff. July 1, 2025.
p3: Y — SF 383 (2025), on/after July 1, 2025.

**What the primary sources show:**
- SF 383 (91st GA), titled "Regulation of Pharmacy Benefit Managers":
  passed Senate 4/28/2025 (36–14), passed House 5/12/2025 (75–15),
  **signed by the Governor 6/11/2025**; enacted as **2025 Iowa Acts ch. 151**.
- Enrolled text amends Iowa Code ch. 510B. New **§ 510B.8(5)**: a PBM
  "shall include any amount paid by a covered person, **or on behalf of a
  covered person**, when calculating the covered person's total contribution
  toward the covered person's cost-sharing." New **§ 510B.8(6)**: amounts
  paid by a covered person for a prescription drug shall be applied to any
  deductible per the plan's coverage documents. **§ 510B.8(7)** carves out
  HSA-qualifying HDHPs until the IRC § 223 minimum deductible is met.
  Sec. 9 applicability: PBMs/health carriers/third-party payors/health
  benefit plans managing a prescription drug benefit in the state on or
  after **July 1, 2025** (some contract provisions key off Jan 1, 2026).

**Links:**
- BillBook (actions/journals): https://www.legis.iowa.gov/legislation/BillBook?ba=SF%20383&ga=91
- Session law text (ch. 151 PDF): https://www.legis.iowa.gov/docs/publications/iactc/91.1/CH0151.pdf

## ND — North Dakota (seed: yes)

**Pass claims:** p1: N (implied — not listed). p2: Y, eff. Jan 1, 2026.
p3: Y — HB 1216 (2025), 2025/2026.

**What the primary sources show:**
- HB 1216 (69th Assembly, 2025), bill no. 25.0068: **signed by the Governor
  4/29/2025**, filed with the Secretary of State 4/29/2025. Enrolled
  version is document 25.0068.05000.
- Text creates a **new section to ch. 26.1-36 NDCC**: defines cost-sharing
  and requires counting of third-party/copay-assistance amounts toward it;
  contains an HSA/IRC § 223 carve-out (requirement does not apply to the
  deductible until the enrollee satisfies the § 223 minimum). Section 2
  amends § 26.1-36.6-03 so that **"Section 1 of this Act applies to a
  self-insurance health plan and is subject to the jurisdiction of the
  commissioner"** — a reach toward self-funded plans that sits in ERISA
  preemption territory (flagging, not resolving).
- Exact effective date: the assembly publishes an "Effective Dates of
  Legislation" PDF (linked below); the passes disagreed (Jan 1, 2026 vs
  2025/2026).

**Links:**

## NE — Nebraska (seed: unknown, pre-existing conflict flag)

**Pass claims:** p1: N (implied). p2: N. p3: Y — Neb. Rev. Stat.
§ 44-4606(5) (via CT OLR).

**What the primary sources show:**
- § 44-4606 **exists and is current** (source note: Laws 2022, LB767, § 6).
  Its title: "Participation contract; requirements; disclosure of
  information; limitations; contract termination; grounds; pharmacy benefit
  manager, prohibited acts."
- Subsection **(5)(a)** — the provision p3 cited — reads: a PBM "shall not
  require a covered person purchasing a covered prescription drug to pay an
  amount greater than the lesser of the covered person's cost-sharing
  amount under the terms of the health benefit plan or the amount the
  covered person would pay for the drug if the covered person were paying
  the cash price." That is a point-of-sale *lesser-of/gag-clause*
  protection, not language about counting third-party assistance toward
  deductibles/OOP.
- The full act index (§§ 44-4601–44-4612, "Pharmacy Benefit Manager
  Licensure and Regulation Act") shows section titles covering licensure,
  contracts, audits, 340B, and specialty networks — no section title
  addressing accumulator adjustment.

**Links:**
- § 44-4606 official text: https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/statutes.php?statute=44-4606
- Act index (44-4601–44-4612): https://nebraskalegislature.gov/laws/search_range_statute.php?begin_section=44-4601&end_section=44-4612

## NV — Nevada (seed: partial)

  TAI/Aimed Alliance page is an advocacy-source account of the guidance,
